Privacy Policy & Data Governance

Your data integrity and privacy are protected at every level of our digital engineering operations.

1. Information Collection

Corva Prime collects contact information (such as name, corporate email address, WhatsApp number, and website URLs) submitted voluntarily via our Strategic Audit and Free Audit request forms.

2. Use of Information

All collected information is strictly used to analyze technical website performance, prepare tailored growth and automation strategy reports, and communicate directly with requesting clients. We never sell or share client data with third-party advertisers.

3. Data Security & Encryption

We employ enterprise TLS/SSL encryption, secure API endpoints, and zero-retention data pipelines to safeguard client information against unauthorized access.

4. Direct Contact

For privacy inquiries or data removal requests, contact executive leadership directly at info@corvaprime.com.
